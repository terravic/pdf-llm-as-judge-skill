date_of_birthstring | req
/^\d{4}-\d{2}-\d{2}$/		PATIENT IDENTIFICATION
Must match the patient's date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format. Must not be confused with specimen collection date, accession date, or report release date.
CONFUSED_WITH_COLLECTION_DATE CONFUSED_WITH_REPORT_DATE FORMAT_MISMATCH

/^MRN-\d{7}$/		PATIENT IDENTIFICATION

/^ACC-2026-MCED-\d{4}$/		SPECIMEN & ORDER INFORMATION

CONFUSED_WITH_MRN FORMAT_MISMATCH HALLUCINATION
ordering_physicianstring | reqSPECIMEN & ORDER INFORMATION
Must extract the ordering clinician's name and credentials from the order section. Must not confuse with laboratory medical director or reviewing pathologist.
LAB_DIRECTOR_CONFUSED_AS_ORDERING_PHYSICIAN PHYSICIAN_NAME_TRUNCATED NPI_CONFUSED_AS_NAME
cancer_signal_statusstring | reqOVERALL CANCER SIGNAL STATUS
Must extract the primary overall cancer signal result classification as stated in the primary result banner.
INCORRECT_CLASSIFICATION HALLUCINATION FORMAT_MISMATCH
primary_cancer_signal_originstring | reqOVERALL CANCER SIGNAL STATUS
Must extract the primary predicted tissue of origin (CSO 1) with highest probability. Must NOT extract secondary or lower-probability signal origins as primary.
SECONDARY_ORIGIN_CONFUSED_AS_PRIMARY HALLUCINATION TEXT_TRUNCATION
primary_origin_probabilitynumber | reqOVERALL CANCER SIGNAL STATUS
Must extract the numeric prediction probability for the primary cancer signal origin as a decimal between 0.0 and 1.0 (convert percentage to decimal if necessary).
SECONDARY_PROBABILITY_EXTRACTED PERCENTAGE_NOT_CONVERTED_TO_DECIMAL VALUE_TRANSPOSITION

MUTATION_VAF_CONFUSED_AS_TUMOR_FRACTION DECIMAL_SHIFT HALLUCINATION

[gene, variant, exon, vaf_pct, tier]		SECTION 1: TARGETED SOMATIC GENOMIC ALTERATIONS
